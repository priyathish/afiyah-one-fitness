About

A trainer who builds habits, not just workouts.

I'm a personal trainer with 8 years of hands-on experience coaching clients in India across every fitness level. I'm now based in Abu Dhabi and hold a UAE-recognised Personal Training Diploma (Level 2 and 3) along with First Aid and CPR-AED certification.

My approach is simple. I assess where you are, build a program around your goal and your physical ability, and track progress on a regular schedule. As you improve, the plan changes with you.

I also hold a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering from Anna University, which shapes how I think about movement and technique.
